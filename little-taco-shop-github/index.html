About LTS

LTS was founded in 2026 by taco lovers who wanted to bring simple, fresh Mexican-inspired food to the neighborhood.

Our shop is small, our menu is focused, and our tacos are made to order. We hope you enjoy every bite!

Taco Trivia

When did tacos become popular?

Tacos have a long history in Mexican cuisine. Their exact origin is debated, but the dish has become one of the world's best-known foods.
